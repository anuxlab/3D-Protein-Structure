# 03 — Companies and Ecosystem

[Back to README](../README.md) | [Previous: Major AI Systems](02-major-ai-systems.md) | [Next: Choosing a Tool](04-choosing-a-tool.md)

The field includes tech giants, specialized AI-native biotech companies, open-source initiatives, and regional players. Capital has flowed heavily into the sector: since 2019, AI drug discovery and protein design have attracted **over $10 billion in cumulative funding**, though investors are increasingly focused on real products and clinical data rather than "AI stories" alone[reference:27].

## Tech giants and subsidiaries

| Organization | Role and recent activity |
|---|---|
| **Google DeepMind** | Created AlphaFold; foundational research and models. AlphaFold2's CASP14 result was a watershed moment, with median GDT_TS above 90[reference:28]. |
| **Isomorphic Labs** | DeepMind spin-off and drug-discovery engine. In February 2026 it released **IsoDDE**, described by some scientists as "**an AlphaFold 4-level advance**"; unlike AlphaFold, it was **not open-sourced**[reference:29]. Isomorphic raised **$2.1 billion** in 2026 to pursue AI-based drug discovery[reference:30]. First AI-designed drugs are targeted for clinical trials around the end of 2026[reference:31]. |
| **OpenAI** | Developing AI systems for biological data analysis and drug-development workflow automation; backed Chai Discovery through its startup fund and participated in later rounds[reference:32]. |
| **NVIDIA** | Infrastructure and cloud services such as **BioNeMo**; enables protein-folding AI deployment. Also an investor in EvolutionaryScale/Biohub-adjacent efforts[reference:33]. |
| **ByteDance / Anew Labs** | Developed **AnewFold** and **Protenix**, an open-source AlphaFold 3 reproduction. |

## Specialized AI biotech startups

| Company | Focus and funding highlights |
|---|---|
| **Chai Discovery** | Founded 2024 by former OpenAI researchers. **Chai-1** (structure prediction) and **Chai-2** (de novo antibody design). Funding trajectory: **$70M Series A (Aug 2025)** → **$130M Series B at $1.3B valuation (Dec 2025)** → **$400M Series C at $3.8B valuation (Jul 2026)**; total haul above $600M[reference:34]. Partners include Lilly, Novartis, and Pfizer[reference:35]. Chai-1 ranked second among nine methods on a 200-complex antibody–antigen benchmark[reference:36]. |
| **Xaira Therapeutics** | Launched in 2024 with **$1 billion** in funding; co-founded by **David Baker** (2024 Nobel laureate) with former Stanford president Marc Tessier-Lavigne as CEO[reference:37]. Has raised **$1.3 billion** to date[reference:38]. Platforms include **X-Design** (antibody design), **X-Cell** (a **4.9-billion-parameter virtual cell model**), and **X-Atlas** datasets (e.g., ~8 million cells in X-Atlas/Orion)[reference:39]. |
| **EvolutionaryScale** | Originator of the **ESM** protein language model series (ESM-2, ESM-3, ESMFold). **Acquired by the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) in November 2025** and folded into **Biohub**, with Alex Rives as Head of Science[reference:40]. The team subsequently released **ESMFold2** and the **ESM Atlas**, mapping **6.8 billion sequences** and a large structure/function landscape[reference:41]. |
| **Generate:Biomedicines** | Generative protein design via the **Chroma** platform. Has raised nearly **$700 million since 2020**, including a **$273 million Series C** with investors including Amgen, NVentures, and ADIA[reference:42]. |
| **Profluent** | LLM-inspired approaches to biological sequences and novel molecular systems. Raised approximately **$150 million**; demonstrated **OpenCRISPR**, showing that generative AI can design functional gene-editing proteins[reference:43]. |
| **Absci** | AI antibody and protein design platform; secured a collaboration with **Merck worth up to $610 million**, a representative case of AI protein design being adopted by large pharma[reference:44]. |
| **Latent Labs** | Generative models for protein design and programmable biology, with a focus on all-atom, epitope-conditioned binder generation. |
| **Nabla Bio** | De novo antibody design with iterative reasoning and testing. |
| **OpenProtein.AI** | Protein foundation models and structural dynamics prediction. |

## Open-source and consortium efforts

| Initiative | Notes |
|---|---|
| **OpenFold** | Non-profit consortium developing open-source AI software for protein structure prediction and design. **OpenFold3** is an open AlphaFold3-family model evaluated in antibody–antigen and binder-discrimination benchmarks[reference:45][reference:46]. |
| **Biohub (CZI)** | Now hosts the former EvolutionaryScale team and has released **ESMFold2** and **ESM Atlas** as open scientific resources for prediction, design, and discovery[reference:47]. |

## Ecosystem summary

- **Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs** pursue foundational models and drug pipelines; Isomorphic's IsoDDE represents a more applied, closed "AlphaFold 4-level" direction[reference:52].
- **Chai Discovery, Xaira Therapeutics, and similar startups** build specialized prediction and design platforms with large funding rounds and pharma partnerships.
- **OpenAI and NVIDIA** shape workflow automation and infrastructure.
- **Open-source efforts** (OpenFold3, ESMFold2, Protenix) improve transparency and reproducibility, and often perform competitively in benchmarks[reference:53][reference:54].
- **The market is shifting** from valuing "AI stories" toward demanding real products and clinical data; the first AI-designed drugs are expected to read out clinically around 2026–2027[reference:55].
