# 03 — Companies and Ecosystem

[Back to README](../README.md) | [Previous: Major AI Systems](02-major-ai-systems.md) | [Next: Choosing a Tool](04-choosing-a-tool.md)

The field includes tech giants, specialized AI-native biotech companies, open-source initiatives, and regional players.

## Tech giants and subsidiaries

| Organization | Role in the ecosystem |
|---|---|
| **Google DeepMind** | Created AlphaFold; foundational research and models |
| **Isomorphic Labs** | DeepMind spin-off focused on AI-driven drug discovery; works with AlphaFold-related technology |
| **OpenAI** | Developing AI systems for biological data analysis and drug-development workflow automation |
| **NVIDIA** | Infrastructure and cloud services such as BioNeMo; enables protein-folding AI deployment |
| **ByteDance / Anew Labs** | Developed AnewFold and Protenix, an open-source AlphaFold 3 reproduction |

## Specialized AI biotech startups

| Company | Focus area |
|---|---|
| **Chai Discovery** | Multimodal foundation models such as Chai-1 for biomolecular complex prediction |
| **Xaira Therapeutics** | Integrated drug discovery engine; antibody design and virtual cell modeling |
| **EvolutionaryScale** | Protein language models including the ESM series |
| **Generate:Biomedicines** | Generative protein design via the Chroma platform |
| **Profluent** | LLM-inspired approaches to biological sequences and novel molecular systems |
| **Latent Labs** | Generative models for protein design and programmable biology |
| **Nabla Bio** | De novo antibody design with iterative reasoning and testing |
| **OpenProtein.AI** | Protein foundation models and structural dynamics prediction |

## Ecosystem summary

- **Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs** pursue foundational models and drug pipelines.
- **Chai Discovery, Xaira Therapeutics, and similar startups** build specialized prediction and design platforms.
- **OpenAI and NVIDIA** shape workflow automation and infrastructure.
- **Open-source efforts** improve transparency and reproducibility.
